## Returns in 30 days

You can send back unused items within 30 days of delivery. Food and anything hygiene-sensitive must be sealed and unopened.

## How it works

1. Contact us with your order number and the items you want to return.
2. We reply with the return steps.
3. When we receive the items, your refund goes back to the original payment method.

## Your right to withdraw

If you are in the EU you may withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason. Use the withdraw from contract form linked in the footer. We confirm it by email with the date and time we received it.
