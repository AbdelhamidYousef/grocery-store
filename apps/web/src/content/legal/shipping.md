## Where we deliver

Berlin only. Enter your postcode at the top of any page to see whether we deliver to you. The delivery page lists the areas and their postcodes.

## Delivery options

- **Slot:** one hour, today or up to six days ahead. 2.99 EUR, plus 1.00 EUR for slots that start between 17:00 and 20:00. Free when your goods come to 49.00 EUR or more.
- **Express:** within 30 minutes where the area allows it. 4.99 EUR, never free.
- Neukölln pays 0.50 EUR more on any delivery. The minimum order is 15.00 EUR of goods.

## Tracking

Once an order is packed you can follow it on the track order page with your order number and email.
