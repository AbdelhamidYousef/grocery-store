## Delivery options

- **Standard:** 4.90 EUR plus a per-kg amount, 3 to 5 working days.
- **Express:** 9.90 EUR plus a per-kg amount, 1 to 2 working days.

Standard shipping is free for orders over 49.00 EUR.

## Where we deliver

The checkout lists the countries available. Choose your country in the address step to see the options and prices for it.

## Tracking

Once an order is packed you can follow it on the track order page with your order number and email.

Nothing is really shipped: this is a demo store.
