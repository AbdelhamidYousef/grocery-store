## What this page is

A short, plain-language privacy notice for a demo store. The store is fictional, so no real personal data should be entered.

## What we collect

- Account details: name, email and a password stored only as a salted hash.
- Order details: items, delivery address and the email used.
- Messages you send us through the contact or withdrawal forms.

## What we do not do

We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. We only set the cookies needed for the session and the cart.

## Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete your data through the contact page.

This text is fictional and is not legal advice.
