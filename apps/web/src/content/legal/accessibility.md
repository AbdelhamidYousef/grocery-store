## Our aim

We want everyone to be able to use Pawsome. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 level AA.

## What we do

- Every page can be used with a keyboard.
- Forms have visible labels and clear error messages.
- Colours are chosen for contrast, and nothing relies on colour alone.
- Images that carry meaning have text descriptions.

## Known gaps

This is a demo and has not had a full external audit. If you find a barrier, tell us through the contact page and we will look into it.
