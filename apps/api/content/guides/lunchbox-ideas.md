A lunchbox is easier when you only have to assemble it.

## The ideas

- Wholemeal toast with cream cheese and sliced cucumber.
- Natural yoghurt with apple cubes and a spoon of oats.
- Pasta from last night with a handful of peas.
- Cheese, apple slices and a few crackers.
- One oat cookie, as the treat at the end.

## A quick tip

Pack the wet things (yoghurt, cucumber) in a separate small pot, so the bread stays crisp.
