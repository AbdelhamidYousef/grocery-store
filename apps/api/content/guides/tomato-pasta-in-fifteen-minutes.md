A plain tomato sauce is the best thing to know how to make on a busy evening.

## You need

- 200 g spaghetti
- 1 tin of chopped tomatoes (400 g)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 pot of fresh basil
- Salt, and a ball of mozzarella if you like

## How to make it

1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water for the time on the pack.
2. Warm the olive oil in a pan, add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt, and simmer for ten minutes.
3. Drain the pasta, stir it into the sauce and tear the basil on top.

## A quick tip

Keep a little of the pasta water. A spoonful makes the sauce cling to the spaghetti.
