A freezer is a way to waste less food. These habits keep it working for you.

## What freezes well

- Bread and rolls, sliced first, for up to three months.
- Berries, spread out on a tray first so they do not stick together.
- Cooked rice and pasta, in portions, for up to a month.

## What to avoid

- Lettuce, cucumber and raw tomatoes. They turn soft when they thaw.
- Refreezing anything that has fully thawed.

## A quick tip

Write the date on the bag. If you cannot remember when you froze it, it is probably time to eat it.
