A good breakfast is mostly good ingredients and a little patience.

## You need

- 6 fresh rolls
- 8 free-range eggs
- Butter and strawberry jam
- A punnet of strawberries

## How to make it

1. Warm the rolls in the oven at 180 degrees for five minutes.
2. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook them for six minutes for a soft yolk.
3. Rinse the cold strawberries and put everything on the table at once.

## A quick tip

Order the rolls with your breakfast shopping. They arrive baked that morning.
