Leaves wilt because they lose water. Keep the water in and they last longer.

## What helps

- Keep basil at room temperature in a glass of water, like flowers. The fridge is too cold for it.
- Wrap lettuce in a slightly damp cloth and put it in the vegetable drawer.
- Keep baby spinach in its bag, and put a folded paper towel inside to catch the moisture.

## A quick tip

Wash leaves just before you eat them, not when you unpack the shopping.
